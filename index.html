OBSERVATIONS FROM THE BEDSIDE
01

Medication workflows shouldn't rely on memory.

When timing, prior administrations, and overlapping orders aren't easy to reconcile, nurses become the system's safety net. I want to design workflows that surface the right information at the right moment.

02

Technology should reduce cognitive load.

Clinicians already make hundreds of decisions in environments full of interruptions. Good technology should organize complexity — not add another layer clinicians have to manage.

03

The workaround is data.

When clinicians create cheat sheets, reminders, extra clicks, or unofficial processes to get their work done, they're showing us exactly where the system isn't meeting the workflow.

04

Adoption starts with the user.

A technically impressive product doesn't matter if it doesn't work in the reality of clinical care. The people using a system every day should help shape how it's designed, deployed, and improved.
